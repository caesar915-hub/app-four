# Product

## Register

product

## Users

Adults with ADHD using iPhone to journal their day. They record voice notes when focus is low or writing feels impossible — on the go, between tasks, or at end of day. Their context is often fragmented attention, variable energy, and a need to track medication timing and how they feel without friction. They are not power users; they want the app to disappear into the habit.

## Product Purpose

Whisper Notes is a private, on-device voice journal that extracts ADHD-relevant signal — mood, energy, focus, medication, sleep, side effects — from natural speech. It surfaces patterns over time through a monthly journal and calendar view. Everything stays on device. Success looks like a user who can record a two-minute voice note and immediately see a structured summary without editing anything.

## Brand Personality

Warm, personal, gentle. A supportive companion that holds your data without judgment. Not a tracker, not a medical tool — a private space to be honest.

## Anti-references

- Clinical / medical apps: cold blue/white, form-heavy, structured like a patient intake form
- Productivity / GTD apps: streaks, gamification, dense checklists, "habit" framing
- Social journaling (Day One, Notion): markdown editing, portfolio-like presentation, sharing-oriented

## Design Principles

1. **The app should disappear into the habit.** Every interaction should feel effortless. If recording a note requires three taps, the user won't do it when their focus is low.
2. **Private by default, visible when useful.** The glass aesthetic and muted palette reflect that this data is personal. Color carries meaning (mood), not decoration.
3. **Show signal, not data.** The goal is insight, not a log. Surface what matters: how was today different from yesterday? What patterns emerge this week?
4. **Gentle, not silent.** Empty states and prompts should feel like a supportive nudge, not a void. Teach and encourage, don't just wait.
5. **Respect limited attention.** Short labels, minimal chrome, no choreography. Users are here to log quickly and leave.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

WCAG AA target. ADHD users may also have comorbid conditions (anxiety, dyslexia, processing differences) — prioritize clear visual hierarchy, reduce motion support, and avoid dense or timed interactions. Dynamic Type support is essential. Color should never be the sole indicator of state.
