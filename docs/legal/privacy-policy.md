## The short version

Squirl is designed to keep your data on your device. Your voice recordings, their
transcripts, and your check-ins are stored locally on your iPhone. **We have no
servers and no backend** — your content is never uploaded to us, and we do not sell,
share, or monetise it. We do not use third-party analytics or advertising SDKs, and
we do not track you across apps or websites.

## What the app accesses, and why

| Data | Why | Where it goes |
|------|-----|---------------|
| **Microphone** | To record audio when you start a recording or voice check-in | Stored only on your device |
| **Speech recognition** | To transcribe your recordings into text | Performed on-device; audio is not sent to us |
| **Recordings & transcripts** | The core of the app — your notes and check-ins | Stored only on your device |
| **Apple Health** *(only if you connect it)* | To show your sleep, activity, heart, and cycle data alongside your check-ins | Read-only; read on-device; never uploaded and never written back to Health |

We request each of these only when the relevant feature needs it, and iOS asks for
your explicit permission first. You can revoke any permission at any time in the iOS
**Settings** app.

## What we collect about you

**Nothing is sent to us.** Squirl has no backend server. Specifically:

- **No accounts.** You do not create an account, and we hold no profile about you.
- **No analytics or tracking.** We do not embed third-party analytics, advertising,
  or tracking SDKs. We do not use the Advertising Identifier (IDFA) and we do not
  ask for App Tracking Transparency permission, because we do not track you.
- **Diagnostics stay on your device.** The app uses Apple's on-device MetricKit to
  surface performance and stability information to you within the app. This data is
  not transmitted to us.

## Apple Health data (HealthKit)

If you choose to connect Apple Health, Squirl reads selected health and cycle data
**read-only** to display it next to your check-ins. In line with Apple's
requirements:

- Health data is read on your device and is **never** transmitted off your device.
- We **never** write data to Apple Health.
- Health data is **never** used for advertising, marketing, or sold to or shared
  with third parties or data brokers.
- Health data is **never** stored in iCloud or any cloud service by Squirl.

You can disconnect Squirl's access to Health at any time in **Settings → Health →
Data Access & Devices**.

## Sharing and exporting — your choices

Some actions let *you* move your own data off the device. These happen only when you
initiate them, and you control the destination:

- **Sharing a transcript or recording** uses the standard iOS share sheet; the
  content goes wherever you send it.
- **Sending a feedback or bug report** (available in test builds) opens your own
  Mail app or the iOS share sheet with details you choose to include. We receive
  only what you decide to send.

## Data retention and deletion

Because your data lives on your device, you are in control of it:

- **Clear All Data** in the app's Settings permanently deletes your recordings and
  check-ins.
- Deleting the app from your iPhone removes its locally stored data.
