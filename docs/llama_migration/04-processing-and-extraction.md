# 04 — Processing & Extraction

Documents branch `main` @ `43eb6515a63a0eca957a79aad257da380c73edd4`. All `path:line` citations are against `main`.

## Purpose

Describe how a captured check-in becomes structured data: on-device Whisper transcription, the pending-transcription queue for recordings captured before the model is ready, the rule/lexicon-based NaturalLanguage extraction pipeline (`NLNoteExtractor`), summarization and persistence, the "Edit check-in" extraction review sheet, provenance tagging, and Whisper model download/delete management.

## Scope

- In scope: `WhisperKitTranscriptionService`, `PendingTranscriptionServiceImpl`, `ProcessingViewModel`, `NLNoteExtractor` + `CueMatcher` + `Lexicon`/`LexiconData`/`lexicon.json`, `NLSummarizationService`, `PersonalLexiconBuilder`, `AIModelServiceImpl`, `ExtractionReviewView`/`ExtractionReviewViewModel`, `RecordingTag`, `Recording.applySummary`/`setMedicationEvents`, the signal enums in `Packages/SquirlSignals`.
- Out of scope: audio capture itself (check-in capture), rendering of extracted data in the library ([Library & History](05-library-and-history.md)), aggregation into insights ([Insights](06-insights.md)), and the medication bar / Dose Guard ([Medications](07-medications.md)).

## Actors & triggers

- **User** — records a voice check-in; taps "Stop & save"; opens the Edit sheet from a recording's detail view; downloads/deletes the Whisper model in settings; taps "Tap to retry" / "Retry transcription" after a failure.
- **System triggers** — app launch (`.task`), every foreground (`scenePhase == .active`), and background model-download completion all trigger a pending-queue drain (`SquirlApp.swift:125-130, 161`). Recording stop triggers transcription when the model is installed; otherwise the recording enters the pending queue. Transcription completion triggers extraction automatically — there is **no** automatic review prompt.

## Functional requirements

### Transcription provider

- **FR-PRC-01 — Single on-device transcription provider.** Transcription is performed by `WhisperKitTranscriptionService`, an `actor` wrapping WhisperKit with a single hard-coded model `openai_whisper-small`. There is no runtime provider switch on `main`: `AppDependencies` wires a single shared instance; `SpeechTranscriptionService` and `MockTranscriptionService` exist but are not wired (`WhisperKitTranscriptionService.swift:7-11`, `AppDependencies.swift`).
- **FR-PRC-02 — Hard-coded English.** `DecodingOptions(language: "en", promptTokens:)` — the language is always English (`WhisperKitTranscriptionService.swift:133`).
- **FR-PRC-03 — ADHD medical prompt, opt-out defaulting ON.** When enabled, transcription is seeded with a fixed medical/ADHD prompt: *"Daily ADHD check-in journal. Today I woke up fine, slept 6 hours, good mood, energy more or less ok but I feel focused. Took the medication two hours ago, Concerta 36mg. Other meds: Vyvanse, Elvanse, Adderall XR, Ritalin, Strattera, Focalin, Dexedrine, Wellbutrin, Modafinil, methylphenidate, lisdexamfetamine, dextroamphetamine, atomoxetine. Hyperfocused, brain fog, executive dysfunction, task paralysis, initiation paralysis, stimming, body doubling, rebound, wearing off, afternoon crash, flat affect, appetite loss, dry mouth."* The opt-out key `UserDefaults.medicalPromptEnabled` **defaults to `true`**; when off, `promptTokens = nil` so non-medical audio isn't dragged toward medication vocabulary (`WhisperKitTranscriptionService.swift:18-24, 130-141`; `Constants.swift:23-38`).
- **FR-PRC-04 — Non-speech marker stripping.** Before persistence, a regex removes literal Whisper non-speech markers — `BLANK AUDIO`, `SILENCE`, `NO SPEECH`, `MUSIC`, `INAUDIBLE`, `NOISE`, `SOUND`, `PAUSE`, `APPLAUSE`, `LAUGH(S)`, `LAUGHTER`, `BEEP`, `STATIC`, `CLICKING` (case-insensitive, bracket or paren, `_`/space variants) — then collapses 2+ whitespace and trims. Real parentheticals such as "[sound of rain]" are left untouched (`WhisperKitTranscriptionService.swift:33-46`).
- **FR-PRC-05 — Streaming lifecycle.** `transcribe(audioURL:)` cancels any prior in-flight transcription (single-inference discipline), yields progress segments **"Setting up on-device transcription…"** (first load only) and **"Transcribing…"** (both `isFinal: false`), holds a `beginBackgroundTask(name: "WhisperTranscription")` for the whole inference, joins all result text, and **unloads the model before finishing the stream** so Metal/CoreML buffers are freed before NL extraction. An empty result throws `AudioConverterError.conversionFailed("No transcription result")`; errors yield a final `isError: true` segment with text `"Transcription error: \(error.localizedDescription)"` (`WhisperKitTranscriptionService.swift:83-214`).
- **FR-PRC-06 — 90-second transcription timeout.** Stream consumption races against a 90 s timeout (`consumeStreamWithTimeout(..., timeoutSeconds: 90)`); on timeout the service transcription is cancelled and the recording is marked `.failed` with text **"Transcription timed out. Tap to retry in the recording detail view."** On cancellation: **"Transcription cancelled. Tap to retry in the recording detail view."** Both are persisted in a fresh, uncancelled MainActor task (`CheckInViewModel.swift:246-330`).
- **FR-PRC-07 — Serialization across recordings.** A new recording's transcription chains after any prior in-flight transcription — the single WhisperKit engine never runs two inferences at once, and a back-to-back recording never cancels the previous transcription (`CheckInViewModel.swift:157-167, 209-214`). The service independently cancels any prior `activeTranscriptionTask` (`WhisperKitTranscriptionService.swift:97`).

### Pending-transcription lifecycle

- **FR-PRC-08 — Pending status.** When a recording is saved but the Whisper model is not installed (`aiModelService.localPath(for: .whisper) == nil`), the recording is persisted with `status = .pendingTranscription` and transcription is skipped. This status is distinct from `.transcribing` and `.failed`: launch-time orphan recovery must **not** sweep it to `.failed`, and the UI shows the calm display title **"Ready shortly…"** — never error language (`CheckInViewModel.swift:200-207`; `AppEnums.swift:4-14`; `Recording.swift:44-51`; `RecordingStore.swift:14-36`).
- **FR-PRC-09 — Drain triggers.** `PendingTranscriptionServiceImpl` (an `actor`) drains the queue on three triggers wired in `SquirlApp`: app launch (`.task`), every foreground transition (`scenePhase == .active`), and background-download completion (`SquirlApp.swift:125-130, 161`; `PendingTranscriptionServiceImpl.swift:4-16`).
- **FR-PRC-10 — Serialized drain.** `drainIfModelReady()` no-ops when the model is absent; an `isDraining` latch coalesces concurrent/re-entrant triggers into a single pass. Pending recording **UUIDs** (not models) are fetched oldest-first (`createdAt` ascending); each recording is re-resolved at drain time, so a recording deleted meanwhile is simply not found. Between recordings the drain re-checks the model still exists (it could be deleted mid-drain) and stops if not (`PendingTranscriptionServiceImpl.swift:37-63`).
- **FR-PRC-11 — Identical pipeline.** Each drained recording runs through the **exact** post-recording path: stream transcription (error segments throw; each segment writes `fullTranscriptText` + `status = .transcribing`) → `Task.checkCancellation()` → `summarizationService.summarize(rawTranscription:)` → `applySummary` + `setMedicationEvents` + `status = .completed`. Cancellation is logged and swallowed (the recording keeps its status); any other error marks `.failed` with `"Transcription failed: \(error.localizedDescription)"` (`PendingTranscriptionServiceImpl.swift:68-144`).

### NL extraction pipeline (`NLNoteExtractor`)

- **FR-PRC-12 — Rule-based, on-device extraction.** Extraction is 100% on-device, rule/lexicon-based NaturalLanguage — **no LLM, no model download** ("Instant; no model download required. iOS 26+ / macOS 15+"). `NLNoteExtractor` is `nonisolated` and `Sendable`; it runs in `Task.detached(priority: .userInitiated)` and checks `Task.isCancelled` between sentences, returning the partial extraction collected so far (`NLNoteExtractor.swift:4-11, 151-153`; `NLSummarizationService.swift:24-29`).
- **FR-PRC-13 — Sentence pipeline.** Empty/whitespace transcripts produce `NoteExtraction(title: "Empty Note")`. Otherwise text is split with `NLTokenizer(unit: .sentence)` (sentences > 3 chars kept; if none, the whole text is one sentence). Whole-text structured regexes (dose, sleep hours, onset minutes, duration hours, crash time, intake context) run **first**, each taking its first match; then a per-sentence loop; then post-loop aggregation (`NLNoteExtractor.swift:101-146, 331-343`).
- **FR-PRC-14 — CueMatcher mechanics.** Tokens are lowercased `NLTokenizer(.word)` surfaces; a token's `verbLemma` is non-nil only when `NLTagger` tagged it `.verb` with a differing lemma (nouns never lemma-match). Multi-word cues match contiguous surface-token runs; single-word cues match exact surface, curated `altForms` (currently an empty dictionary — the `NLTagger` lemma model is absent on the iOS simulator), or the verb-lemma fallback. Disambiguation is **longest-match-wins** ("not bad" beats "bad"; "feel nothing" beats "nothing"). Tokenization deliberately uses `NLTokenizer` boundaries, not NLTagger word enumeration, so "doctor's" cannot spuriously match the "doctor" cue. Lemma matching is disabled for side effects, physical side effects, rebound, appetite loss/return, appointments, and all activity keywords (polysemous gerunds like "reading"→"read" caused false positives); mood/energy/focus/emotions/tasks/wins/overwhelm/executive-dysfunction/physical-stim use the lemma fallback (`CueMatcher.swift:12-199`; `NLNoteExtractor.swift:59-95`).
- **FR-PRC-15 — Negation.** Each matched cue is checked for negation: **"no" requires a window of 1** (immediately preceding word); all other negation tokens use a **window of 5** words. `"n't"` matches any word with suffix "n't"; others require exact equality. The token set is exactly `["not", "never", "no", "n't", "without"]`. Negated values are flipped asymmetrically — `flipMood`: great/good→low, low→okay, okay→low, flat→okay; `flipEnergy`: charged→sluggish, alert→tired, steady→tired, tired→steady, sluggish→alert; `flipFocus`: lockedIn→foggy, sharp→distracted, present→distracted, distracted→present, foggy→sharp (`NLNoteExtractor.swift:351-398`; `Lexicon.swift:362-364`).
- **FR-PRC-16 — Tense weighting and aggregation.** Each mood candidate carries its sentence's `temporalWeight` (present **1.0** / neutral **0.5** / past **0.2**, from `TenseClassifier`); the winner is max by (weight, then most recent sentence index). Mood = present-tense-wins; energy and focus = strongest-match-wins (longest matched phrase). There is **no sentiment fallback** — NLTagger paragraph sentiment was removed as too negatively biased on short factual text. All list fields are deduped and **sorted alphabetically**; sideEffects and triage lists contain **whole sentences**, not just matched cues (`NLNoteExtractor.swift:258-306`; `TenseClassifier.swift:15-21`).
- **FR-PRC-17 — Gated weak fallbacks.** Weak energy descriptors are consulted only when the sentence contains "energy" or "energetic" (default `.steady`, phrase "energy"); weak mood descriptors only when the sentence contains "mood" (default "okay"). An "energy drink/bar/gel/shot/balls" product regex returns nil — a product is never a self-report. Weak candidates go through the same negation/flip and tense-weighting as strong ones; each pool is used only when no strong cue of that signal matched (`NLNoteExtractor.swift:443-513`).
- **FR-PRC-18 — Medication extraction.** Per sentence: (1) exact substring hits against ~89 lexicon med names (bundled `lexicon.json`; ~55 in code defaults); (2) a **fuzzy typo fallback** — only when zero exact hits **and** med context is present (a dose regex match or any of {took, take, taking, taken, dose, skipped, forgot, missed, mg, milligram, milligrams}) — testing each token of length **≥ 6** (minus stoplist {concert, concerts, concerto}) with Damerau-Levenshtein **edit distance exactly 1** against single-token med names; (3) overlap dedup (a longer different-name hit suppresses a shorter one — "Concerta XL" beats "Concerta", "dextroamphetamine" beats "amphetamine"); (4) clause scoping at " and ", " but ", " then ", " so ", ", " so each med's dose/time/quantity/change comes only from its own clause. `taken = !(negated || notTaken)`; `medNotTaken` walks tokens backward from the med, matching medication-specific verbs `["forgot", "missed", "skipped", "skip", "forget"]` before any clause-break token — so "I skipped lunch and took my Concerta" does **not** mark Concerta missed (`NLNoteExtractor.swift:547-697`; `Lexicon.swift:370-372`; `LexiconData.swift`).
- **FR-PRC-19 — Med dose/time/quantity/change fields.** Dose: regex `\b\d+(?:\.\d+)?\s?(mg|mcg|milligrams?)\b`, "milligram(s)" normalized to "mg". Time: first `NSDataDetector` date match containing a digit → `"HH:mm"` 24-hour + raw `timeLabel`; else `timeOfDayKeywords` fallback → `(nil, keyword)`. Quantity: "half a/of/my/the", "halved", "split/splitting", or "half" + pill/tablet/dose → 0.5; else nil (= 1.0). Change: stop terms → `.stopped`, start terms → `.started`, else nil; "finished" is deliberately omitted (ambiguous with tasks) (`NLNoteExtractor.swift:657-722`).
- **FR-PRC-20 — Sleep detection.** O(1) word-set membership against ~30 sleep words (word-boundary matched) or a "lay/lie awake" / "<n> hours … night" proximity regex. Per-sentence hours accept 1–2 digit or spelled-out (one…twelve) numbers + optional " and a half" + unit hours?/hrs?/hr (a lone "h" is dropped). `hoursGovernedByActivityVerb` rejects durations governed by non-sleep verbs (worked, ran, drove, spent, study, played, walked, cycled, commuted, travelled, hiked…) skipping filler words — "couldn't sleep, so I worked 12 hours" yields nil. Quality: good list → "good", bad list → "poor", insomnia list → "insomnia" (priority order). Whole-text hours regex (trigger word + number + unit) wins over per-sentence; a whole-text hit implies sleep-mentioned even with no keyword sentence (`NLNoteExtractor.swift:516-533, 737-815`).
- **FR-PRC-21 — Highlight extraction (exact thresholds).** Extractive salience scoring per sentence: medications **+3.0**; task completion/avoidance, side-effect/physical-side-effect, executive dysfunction **+2.0**; win, rebound **+2.5**; energy, focus, mood/emotions, appetite **+1.5**; time-of-day **+1.0**. Length: 40–200 chars **+1.0**, < 20 chars **−1.0**. Qualification **threshold = 1.5**; if none qualify, the single highest-scoring sentence is kept; else top N where `topN = min(5, max(1, sentences.count / 4 + 1))`, kept in original order. Dedup: a selected sentence whose token-set **Jaccard overlap exceeds 0.6** with an already-kept higher-scored one is dropped. Med coverage: if any sentence mentions a medication but none survived, the best med sentence is swapped in for the lowest-scored survivor (`NLNoteExtractor.swift:820-904`).
- **FR-PRC-22 — Title generation.** Source = first highlight, else the whole text; cut at the first `,`/`;`/` and then `/` but `.`; 12 spoken fillers stripped (so, yeah, um, uh, like, okay, ok, well, anyway, right, honestly, basically); first **8 words** (fallback: first **6 words** of raw text if empty); first letter capitalized (`NLNoteExtractor.swift:907-928`).

### Extracted signals inventory

- **FR-PRC-23 — Signal schema.** `NoteExtraction` carries: `mood` (label ∈ {low, flat, okay, good, great}), `energy`, `focus` (enum rawValues), `emotions` — **exactly the 20 curated Mood-Meter emotions, 5 per valence×energy quadrant** (excited, joyful, proud, thrilled, inspired; angry, anxious, frustrated, irritated, jealous; content, grateful, peaceful, secure, serene; sad, lonely, disappointed, hopeless, discouraged) — `activities` (11 surface-only categories: Resting, Hobbies, Hanging Out, Fitness, Eating, Driving, Work, Chores, Errands, Outdoors, Screen Time), `medications: [MedEvent]`, `sideEffects`, `sleep: SleepNote`, `tasksCompleted`/`tasksAvoided`/`wins`/`overwhelm`/`highlights`, `title`, triage arrays (`executiveDysfunction`, `physicalStim`, `physicalSideEffects`, `reboundTerms`, `appetiteLoss`, `appetiteReturn`, `appointments`), and regex fields (`extractedDose`, `sleepHours`, `onsetMinutes`, `durationHours`, `crashTime`, `intakeContext`) (`NoteExtraction.swift:4-131`; `Lexicon.swift:166-395`).
- **FR-PRC-24 — Signal scales.** All four signals are 5-step ordinals (`numericValue` 1–5) in the leaf module `Packages/SquirlSignals`: `MoodLevel` low/flat/okay/good/great; `EnergyLevel` sluggish/tired/steady/alert/charged; `FocusLevel` foggy/distracted/present/sharp/lockedIn; `SleepLevel` restless/light/okay/good/deep. `MoodLevel`, `EnergyLevel`, `FocusLevel` conform to the shared `SignalLevel` protocol (displayLabel, color, gradients); `SleepLevel` does not (`Levels.swift:8-99`; `SignalLevel.swift:25-64`).
- **FR-PRC-25 — Whole-text structured regexes.** Compiled once; each returns its first match across the whole text: dose `(\d{1,3})\s?(mg|mcg|milligram)` + optional formulation suffix (XL|XR|IR|SR|ER|ODT|LA|PM|modified/extended/immediate release) + optional frequency; sleep hours (trigger word + number + hours?|hrs?|h); onset minutes (kick-in/onset phrase + number + minutes?|mins?|m|hours?|hrs?, hours ×60 — bare "took" deliberately excluded); duration hours (lasted|duration|worked for|good for|wore off after|wearing off after|coverage + number + unit); crash time (crash|rebound|wore off|dropped off|hit a wall|symptoms came back + optional at/around/about + time — returns the whole matched text, e.g. "crash at 3pm"); intake context (took|had|with|on + empty stomach|full stomach|with food|with breakfast|with lunch|with dinner|after eating|before eating|orange juice|coffee|vitamin C|OJ|protein) (`NLNoteExtractor.swift:936-1041`).

### Summarization & persistence

- **FR-PRC-26 — Summarization mapping.** `NLSummarizationService.summarize(rawTranscription:)` never throws in practice: `bullets` = highlights; `generatedTitle` = extraction title; `mood`/`energyLevel`/`focusLevel` = extraction scalars; `sleepEvent` built only when `sleep.mentioned == true`. Persisted `sideEffects` are the lexicon cue keywords (sideEffectCues + physicalSideEffects, deduped preserving order) that appear as substrings in the extracted sentences — **not** whole sentences. Topics: "Medications" if any med events; "Symptoms" if sideEffects/physicalSideEffects/reboundTerms/appetiteLoss non-empty; "Appointments" if appointments non-empty — appetiteReturn, executiveDysfunction and emotions produce no topics, and `.procedures`/`.general` are never emitted (`NLSummarizationService.swift:33-103`).
- **FR-PRC-27 — SleepLevel derivation.** Quality "insomnia" or "poor" → `.restless`; "good" → `.good`. Otherwise by hours: `<5` → restless; `5..<6` → light; `6..<7` → okay; `7..<9` → good; `≥9` → deep. Nil when sleep wasn't mentioned or neither quality nor hours is known (`NLSummarizationService.swift:55-75`).
- **FR-PRC-28 — Pipeline trigger & status.** `ProcessingViewModel.processRawTranscription` sets `summaryStatus = generating`, calls summarize; on throw → `summaryStatus = failed` and the UI shows **"Tap to retry"**; on success → `recording.applySummary(result)` + `recording.setMedicationEvents(...)` (`ProcessingViewModel.swift:27-92`).
- **FR-PRC-29 — `applySummary` write rules.** Default (voice) mode: title = "Mood · Energy · Focus" parts capitalized (e.g. "Good · Alert · Sharp"; focus uses `displayLabel`) when any exist, else `generatedTitle`; overwrites mood/energy/focus/sleep scalars and `medicationInfo`. `fillOnly` (text check-in) fills only nil scalars — user-entered values are never overwritten. `noteExtractionJSON` is persisted with mood/energy/focus/emotions/sideEffects/sleepHours **stripped** (they live in scalar columns — single-source-of-truth). `summary` = bullets joined as `"- …"` lines; sets `summaryStatus = completed`, `summaryGeneratedAt`, `updatedAt`. `medicationInfo` per med: `name [change] [dose] [at timeLabel] [missed]`, joined "; " (`Recording.swift:202-306`).
- **FR-PRC-30 — Transcript medication events.** `setMedicationEvents` deletes all `source == .transcript` `MedicationEvent`s and recreates them from the extraction, **skipping names that exist as `source == .manual`** (manual dose beats extractor). Duration resolution: per-med `durationHours` ?? call-site default ?? **10.0 h**. Sets `hasMedication` from inputs (`Recording.swift:301-355`). See [Medications](07-medications.md) for `resolvedTakenAt` fuzzy-time mapping.

### Extraction review ("Edit check-in")

- **FR-PRC-31 — Manual-only review entry.** The review sheet is reachable **only** from `RecordingDetailView`'s trailing pencil toolbar button (accessibility label **"Edit check-in"**), presented as a `.sheet(item:)` with `.presentationDetents([.large])` and a visible drag indicator. There is **no automatic review prompt after extraction** — results are applied silently; review is a manual edit affordance on any existing check-in (`RecordingDetailView.swift:54-74`; `ExtractionReviewView.swift:53-54`).
- **FR-PRC-32 — Editable cards.** Six cards: **When** (date and time `DatePicker`s bounded `in: ...Date()` — future dates/times cannot be set); **Signals** (three `GlyphRampPicker` rows MOOD/ENERGY/FOCUS with a current-value line "Name · subtitle", e.g. "Great · bright, thriving"); **Sleep** (all five `SleepLevel` chips toggle, duration presets **2/4/6/8/10 h** rendered "2h"…"10h", plus a custom-hours field, placeholder **"7.5"**, comma→dot normalization); **Medications** (header caption "Stimulants · no limit", footer "Tap to add · expands inline · × to remove · independent events"; per-dose Taken/Missed toggle, catalog dose chips, read-only time line, editable duration field with literal "shortest" caption; time is **not** editable in this sheet); **Emotions** (the 20 curated emotions as "Pleasant"/"Unpleasant" chip groups); **Side effects** (15 hard-coded chips: dry mouth, headache, nausea, appetite gone, insomnia, jittery, heart racing, stomach ache, dizzy, irritable, rebound, crash, sweating, grinding teeth, flat affect) (`ExtractionReviewView.swift:118-440`).
- **FR-PRC-33 — Confirm semantics.** On Save: `noteExtraction` is rebuilt from the edited scalars so persisted JSON can't disagree with columns; `recording.createdAt = date` is set **before** materializing med events so `takenAt` resolves against the corrected day; `applySummary` + `setMedicationEvents` run; a user-set non-empty trimmed title always wins (empty does not overwrite); `updatedAt = Date()`. Cancel: if `summaryStatus != completed`, sets it to `failed` and saves (usually a no-op from the detail path) (`ExtractionReviewViewModel.swift:183-263`).
- **FR-PRC-34 — Provenance tags.** On confirm, one `RecordingTag` is written per mood (if non-empty), energy, focus, each medication, and each emotion: `source: .userCorrected` if that category was touched, else `.nlp`. `TagCategory` covers only mood/energy/focus/medication/emotions — sleep and side-effect edits produce no tags, and their setters don't record edits. `RecordingTag.confidence` is never set (always nil on main); `TagSource` also has a `user` case unused here. Tags are persisted via `store.addCorrectionTags` + `store.save()` (`ExtractionReviewViewModel.swift:35, 231-253`; `RecordingTag.swift:4-43`).

### Personal lexicon (implemented, dormant in 1.0)

- **FR-PRC-35 — Personal lexicon overlay — Implemented, dormant in 1.0.** `PersonalLexiconBuilder.build(from:)` fetches all `RecordingTag`s with `source == "userCorrected"` and extends the lexicon with **medication names** (original casing, deduped case-insensitively) and **emotions** (lowercased) only — mood/energy/focus corrections are value changes, not new phrases. `LexiconData.toLexicon(personalOverlay:)` and `LexiconLoader.loadBundled(overlay:)` accept the overlay, and `NLSummarizationService(personalOverlay:)` consumes it. However, nothing on `main` calls the builder: production wiring uses `NLSummarizationService()` with no overlay (`AppDependencies.swift:27`). The learning loop is built but **not wired into the production pipeline** (`PersonalLexiconBuilder.swift:13-44`; `NLSummarizationService.swift:12-18`; `LexiconData.swift:63-129`).

### Whisper model management

- **FR-PRC-36 — Model download.** `AIModelServiceImpl.download(.whisper)` returns an `AsyncThrowingStream<Double, Error>` of progress via `WhisperKit.download(variant: "openai_whisper-small", downloadBase: ModelConstants.whisperDownloadBase)` (base = `~/Library/whisperkit`); progress is clamped to `min(fractionCompleted, 0.99)` until done, then yields exactly `1.0`. On success: `isDownloaded = true`, `isCorrupted = false`. **Cancellation finishes quietly with no write-back** (the ModelContext may already be torn down). On failure: `isCorrupted = true`, error classified (`AIModelServiceImpl.swift:22-60, 125-131`).
- **FR-PRC-37 — Error classification.** `URLError.dataNotAllowed` → `.cellularDisabled`; `.notConnectedToInternet`/`.networkConnectionLost`/`.cannotConnectToHost`/`.timedOut` → `.noNetwork`; file-write out-of-space/read-only or POSIX `ENOSPC` → `.insufficientSpace`; anything else → `.other(String)` with only a short type tag — content-free by design ("never any transcript or medication data") (`AIModelServiceImpl.swift:65-86`; `Protocols.swift:138-147`).
- **FR-PRC-38 — Delete & ground truth.** `delete(_:)` removes the `whisperDownloadBase` directory and resets `isDownloaded`/`isCorrupted`. `localPath(for:)` walks the download base: a valid folder is named `openai_whisper-small` AND contains `AudioEncoder.mlmodelc/weights` AND `config.json`. **The filesystem is the source of truth**; SwiftData `isDownloaded` mirrors it (`AIModelServiceImpl.swift:88-109, 133-153`).
- **FR-PRC-39 — Metadata.** `ensureMetadata` creates `ModelMetadata(modelName: "openai_whisper-small", modelType: "whisper", modelSize: 74_000_000)` if missing. (The entity's own *defaults* — modelName "base", version "openai-whisper-base-v1" — predate the model switch.) Status enums: `ModelStatus { notInstalled, downloading, installing, ready, corrupted }`; `DownloadStatus { notInstalled, downloading, installed, failed }`; `AIModelType` has the single case `.whisper` (`AIModelServiceImpl.swift:113-123`; `ModelMetadata.swift:4-34`; `AppEnums.swift:45-51, 99-108`).

## User flows

### Happy path — voice check-in to structured data

1. User records a voice note and taps "Stop & save"; the recording is persisted immediately (`.done` / "Captured." shown before transcription starts).
2. Whisper model is installed → background transcription streams (chained after any prior transcription; 90 s timeout); partial text overwrites `fullTranscriptText`, status `.transcribing`.
3. On completion: status `.completed`, then `processRawTranscription` runs extraction; `applySummary` sets title/scalars/JSON columns and `setMedicationEvents` materializes transcript-sourced dose events.
4. The check-in appears in the library and detail views with signals, tags, bullets and medications (see [Library & History](05-library-and-history.md)).

### Alternate flows

- **Model not installed at save time:** recording persisted as `.pendingTranscription` ("Ready shortly…"); drained on next launch/foreground/download-completion through the identical path (FR-PRC-08…11).
- **Transcription timeout/cancel/error:** status `.failed` with retry copy stored in `fullTranscriptText`; user retries from the recording detail "Retry transcription" button (90 s timeout again; success regenerates the summary) — see [Library & History](05-library-and-history.md).
- **User corrects extraction:** opens detail → pencil → Edit check-in → adjusts fields → Save. Scalars rewritten, provenance tags re-emitted with `.userCorrected` for touched categories (FR-PRC-33/34).
- **Lexicon resource missing/malformed:** `LexiconLoader` silently falls back to code defaults (`Lexicon()`) — extraction never breaks (`LexiconData.swift:119-129`).

## UI states

- **Empty transcript** → extraction titled "Empty Note" (`NLNoteExtractor.swift:102-105`).
- **Transcribing** → status `.transcribing`; detail transcript card shows spinner + "Transcribing…".
- **Pending transcription** → status `.pendingTranscription`, display title "Ready shortly…", no pill in the transcript card, no error language.
- **Extraction failure** → `summaryStatus = failed`, UI offers "Tap to retry".
- **Model states** → `ModelStatus` notInstalled/downloading/installing/ready/corrupted; download progress clamped to 0.99 until the final 1.0.
- **Edit sheet** → always populated (seeded from the recording); no empty/loading/error states exist in that view.

## Edge cases

- Cancellation mid-extraction returns partial results (`NLNoteExtractor.swift:151-153`); cancelled model downloads finish quietly without touching possibly torn-down SwiftData state.
- Legacy `noteExtractionJSON` (pre-`appointments`) still decodes via `decodeIfPresent`; `Recording` decodes with `try?`, so any decode failure nulls the JSON extraction without crashing (`NoteExtraction.swift:95-130`).
- "Energy drink/bar/gel/shot" never yields an energy level; "doctor's" never matches the "doctor" appointment cue; "I skipped lunch and took my Concerta" doesn't mark Concerta missed; "couldn't sleep, worked 12 hours" doesn't log 12 h of sleep.
- Recording deleted mid-transcription/mid-drain: the pipeline re-verifies existence and never touches a freed `@Model`.
- No numeric confidence is ever produced; `RecordingTag.confidence` stays nil — disambiguation is rule-based (longest-match-wins, tense weights, gated fallbacks).
- Review-sheet cancel on an incomplete recording marks the summary `failed`.

## Acceptance criteria

1. A completed voice check-in yields persisted mood/energy/focus/sleep scalars, highlights, topics, side effects, emotions, and transcript-sourced medication events without any network call or LLM.
2. A recording saved with the model absent shows "Ready shortly…" and is transcribed+summarized automatically after the model download completes or on next foreground.
3. "Not bad" matches "not bad" (not "bad"); "I'm not feeling great" flips to low mood; present-tense mood beats past-tense.
4. "Took my Conerta 36mg" (one typo, med context present) resolves to Concerta; "concert tonight" never matches.
5. Saving corrections in the Edit sheet rewrites scalars and emits `.userCorrected` tags for exactly the touched categories; untouched categories keep `.nlp`.
6. Model download reports monotonic progress ending at exactly 1.0; deleting the model resets both flags and makes `localPath(for:)` return nil.
