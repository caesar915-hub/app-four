Privacy Policy

Squirl — Last updated: 27 June 2026

Squirl is a voice-first daily check-in app. This policy explains what data the app uses and how it is handled.

What data the app uses

  • Microphone audio — recorded when you start a check-in. Audio is stored locally on your device and is never transmitted to any server.
  • Transcriptions and signals — your speech is transcribed on-device using WhisperKit. Mood, energy, focus, sleep, medication names, and feelings are extracted from the transcription on-device using Apple's Natural Language framework. None of this processing involves external servers or third parties.
  • App preferences — settings such as medication context and display options are stored in UserDefaults on your device.

What we do not collect

  • No data is sent to any server, cloud service, or third party.
  • No analytics, advertising identifiers, or crash reporters are used.
  • No account or profile is created.
  • We do not have access to your recordings, transcriptions, or any signal data.

Data export

You can export your journal from Settings. The export file is encrypted and stays on your device. You control where it goes.

Data deletion

All data is stored locally. Deleting the app removes all data permanently. Individual recordings can be deleted from within the app.

Children

Squirl is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect data from them.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes materially, the updated date above will reflect it. Check this page for the current version.
