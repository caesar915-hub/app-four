A check-in's mood is the headline; energy · focus sit just under it; medication (name + active %) gets a purple line; and the remaining inputs — feelings, side effects, sleep, topics — are chips directly beneath that entry. Nothing is pooled at the day level anymore.

What changed

Before (wrong) All check-ins' inputs were merged into one pill row at the BOTTOM of the day card: “⚡ alert energy · 🌙 8h sleep · 🩹 Headache · ❤️”. You couldn't tell the headache and the 8h sleep belonged to the 16:36 entry vs any other.
After (v8) Each entry carries its own inputs. The 09:15 LOW check-in shows ITS feelings, side effects, sleep, topic and dose — right under it. The 13:40 GOOD check-in shows only what it captured. A med-only node (11:00) stays a hollow bead with just the dose.

Header group = mood · energy · focus · medication. Under-group (chips) = feelings · side effects · sleep · topics. This mirrors Recording.displayTags, just rendered per-row instead of aggregated per-day.
