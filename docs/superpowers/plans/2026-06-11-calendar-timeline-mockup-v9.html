Rules in this mockup

  • Headline = mood + energy + focus, uppercased, one line. Missing parts are dropped (e.g. just “LOW”).
  • TAKEN CONCERTA 36MG = a dose logged at this instant (its 0% ring isn't spelled out — it was just taken).
  • 💊 Concerta 36mg · 40% = an earlier dose still inside its effect window here (ring + %).
  • Med-only node (no voice note) uses “TAKEN …” as its title, on a hollow bead.
  • Chips under each entry = that check-in's feelings · side effects · sleep · topics. Nothing is pooled at the day level.
