Time stays in the bead; inputs stay as text chips. These three vary a different axis each: a visible mood-colour cue, the mood word treatment, and time placement. Same day across all three.

What's varying (everything else is the current look)

  • E · Mood accent bar — each entry becomes a small white card with a solid mood-colour stripe on the left. This is the “tint” idea made obvious — you read the day's emotional arc down the coloured edges. Trade-off: looser, more card-like than a tight timeline.
  • F · Mood pill headline — the mood word is a filled colour pill (GREAT / LOW); energy·focus follow as quiet grey text. Mood reads as a clear tag and the headline stops shouting in all-caps. Trade-off: two type styles per line.
  • G · Agenda gutter — the time moves to a left column like a calendar/agenda; the bead shrinks to a mood dot (keeps its med rings). Time is very readable and the layout feels more “schedule”. Trade-off: smaller mood presence.
