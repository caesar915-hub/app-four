Trade-offs & recommendation

A · Three signalsStrongest "one glance, whole month" view; degrades gracefully with few check-ins (beads just stay gray). Cheap to compute. Recommend: yes — lead section.
B · AveragesCalm summary, beautiful at the top, but averages hide variance (a low+great month reads "okay"). Good companion, weak alone.
C · Daily rhythmThe most actionable for ADHD self-knowledge (med timing, scheduling deep work). Replaces "Your mood during the day" — same axis, three signals instead of one. Recommend: yes — replaces time-of-day bars.
D · ConnectionsHighest perceived intelligence, but needs data thresholds and careful copy to avoid claiming causation. Phase 2 candidate — design now, build after TestFlight.
