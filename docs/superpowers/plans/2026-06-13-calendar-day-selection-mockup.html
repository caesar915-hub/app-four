Calendar — day-selection & navigation variants

Porting Todoist’s collapsible calendar onto our mood/med timeline, reframed for a journal. Live & interactive: drag the grey handle, tap the month label, swipe weeks, tap any day, and scroll the list — the calendar and list stay in sync.

LOCKEDPast, newest-first — opens on Today; scroll down = older days. Future days are dimmed & non-tappable.   LOCKEDEmpty days shown — every calendar day maps to a list anchor (header + “No check-ins”), so tap-to-scroll is exact.
Comparing two axes → columns = how week↔month expands (drag handle · tap chevron · week-only); rows = the day marker (mood-colour dot vs neutral grey dot).   Selected day = solid ink circle (neutral chrome, never competes with mood). Today = ringed. Mood scale: low · flat · okay · good · great.
