Palette combinations — expanded

8 mood × 8 energy × 8 focus ramps = 512 raw combinations, filtered to those with no hue clash between mood, energy, focus, or the fixed medication purple. Clash = any chromatic step within the threshold° of another signal's step on the hue wheel — computed live from the hex values, not hand-picked. Each card shows its headroom (the tightest gap between its signals): bigger = safer.
