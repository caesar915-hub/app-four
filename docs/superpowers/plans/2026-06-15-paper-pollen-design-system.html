03 · Signals

Four dimensions, one glyph language

Each signal has its own shape + hue + fill — colorblind- and grayscale-safe. Mood = sprout (default; selectable, see 03b) · Energy = lightning · Focus = aperture · Sleep = bed icon (ramp deferred) · Medication = capsule.

03b · Mood icon (yours to choose)

One signal, five ways to grow

Mood is the one glyph the user can personalize — a small, no-pressure bit of ownership in Settings → Mood icon. The shipped set is five botanical "open/close" forms that all read 1→5; Sprout is the default. Energy / Focus / Sleep / Medication icons are fixed.

Settings → Mood icon · tap to choose (shown at level 4)

04 · Medication

Purple, a capsule, a bar that fills and never alarms

Purple is the one hue no signal uses. The bar fills from empty (just taken) to full (worn off) over the dose window — one consistent purple, onset pulse, no red, no nagging.

07 · Recording detail & the Edit sheet

Tap "Edit check-in" → one scrolling sheet

Detail loses the back button (swipe-left) and the pencil (→ the "Edit check-in" button). The Edit sheet is a single screen: When · Mood · Energy · Focus · Sleep · Medications · Feelings · Side effects. Mood/Energy/Focus pickers show name + synonym; Sleep drops synonyms + adds custom hours; Medications are Stimulants-only, multi-select, removable, each expanding inline (P1).

08 · Decisions

Everything locked today

IdentityPaper & Pollen — warm paper/loam, organic, calm + frictionless. North star: effortless.
TypeFraunces (hero) · DM Sans (UI/body) · IBM Plex Mono / DM Sans tabular (data).
PaletteShipped ramps kept: Mood Meadow·Burnt · Energy Lemon · Focus Voltage blue. Sleep deferred.
GlyphsMood sprout · Energy lightning · Focus aperture · Sleep bed icon · Medication capsule.
MotionCrescent breathe → revolve+glow → settle-to-check. No confetti, no streaks. Reduce Motion honored.
Check-inIdle (Speak primary) → Listening (nudge + timer + stop) → Saved (Captured, Done leads, no machinery).
Type-noteLayout A — signals-first glyph pickers + note field.
CalendarUnchanged (your favorite).
DetailNo back button (swipe-left); pencil → "Edit check-in" button; audio player last.
Edit sheetOne scroll. Mood/Energy/Focus = glyph pickers with name + synonym; Sleep = named scale no synonyms + custom-hours input; Medications = Stimulants only, multi-select, removable, P1 inline-expand (dose from table · default time info · duration text box defaulted to shortest); independent med events, no count limit.
MedicationCapsule · single purple #7E5CA8 · bar fills empty→full over the dose · no alarm.
Mood iconUser-selectable (Settings → Mood icon): Sprout (default) · Seedling · Flower · Daisy · Clover — five botanical "open/close" forms. Energy/Focus/Sleep/Medication icons fixed.
PostureGlyphs not emoji · no gamification/streaks · meds never nag.
