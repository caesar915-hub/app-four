The Bud glyph language

DECIDED · Bud botanical

One plant world for the three self-state signals: Mood = sprout, Energy = seed pod, Focus = seedhead. Replaces the old sprout/lightning/aperture set — the lightning and aperture came from unrelated visual worlds (weather, optics) and the aperture miscued as a camera. Bud makes the three read as one designed family while every glyph keeps its 1→5 ramp encoded by shape + hue + fill. This doc is the source for the SwiftUI build; it carries the locked self-state glyphs, the Sleep (bed) and Medication (horizontal capsule) icons, and every view they live in.

4 Decisions log (this exploration)

DecisionStatusWhy
Botanical lifecycle as the glyph languagedecidedOne plant world makes the set read as a designed family, not three borrowed symbols.
Bud variant — sprout / pod / seedheaddecidedOwner pick from 5 botanical variants (Bud · Bloom · Spiral · Leaf · Seed). Calm, vertical, cleanest.
Energy: lightning bolt → seed poddecidedBolt was the only conventional/elemental mark in an organic system; pod swells→bursts to encode level.
Focus: aperture → seedheaddecidedAperture miscued as a camera and was mechanical/optical; seedhead packs tighter to encode level.
Mood: sproutkeptAlready botanical and on-brand; unchanged.
Sleep = bed icon (cool indigo)keptFunctionally distinct from the self-state signals; literal bed, ramp deferred. Owner call.
Medication = horizontal capsule (purple)keptReads instantly as a dose; two-tone horizontal capsule, chip not a ramp. Owner call.
