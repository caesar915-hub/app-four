Food + exercise as check-ins on the unfolded Day card

Follow-up to 2026-07-05-nutrition-signals-daycard: folded state is locked to V3 (kcal + caffeine tokens). The unfolded card is re-imagined so food and exercise are their own timeline entries by hour, interleaved with mood check-ins, instead of one per-day footer. Three treatments below; all keep the day totals at the foot and the teal nutrition hue (glyph carries the food/exercise distinction, color is never the only cue). Sample day: breakfast 07:40 · coffee 10:30 · lunch 13:05 · run 18:00 · dinner 20:15, mood check-ins 09:15 / 12:40 / 16:12.
