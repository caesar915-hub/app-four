app-four · Squirl · Low-fi Wireframes

Every view of the app

Black-and-white, low-fidelity wireframes of the complete app: the tab shell, onboarding, and all four tabs (Calendar, Check-in, Insights, Settings) plus the shared Recording Detail, the Edit sheet, every sheet/alert/dialog, and the global medication bar. Structure, element order, copy, and metrics are taken 1:1 from the SwiftUI source. Frames are drawn at 393 pt device width. Colour is intentionally omitted: a tint that carries meaning in-app is shown as grey with a note.

Grounded in the codebase (read this session by 4 extraction passes). Tokens: Spacing xs 4 · s 8 · m 12 · l 16 · xl 20 · xxl 24 · section 32 · hero 40 · Radius card 16 / control 10 / button 16 / chip 15 · minTapTarget 44 · crescentDiameter 300. Signal glyphs are hand-drawn SwiftUI Shapes (Sprout = mood, Bolt = energy, Aperture = focus, Bed = sleep, Capsule = medication), not SF Symbols. They are drawn here as icon placeholders. Sample content uses the real level vocab and lexicon, never invented data.
