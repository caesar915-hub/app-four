Calendar Library

Black-and-white, low-fidelity wireframes of the Calendar tab (CalendarLibraryView), one frame per state and user story. Layout, element order, and metrics are taken 1:1 from the SwiftUI source (frames are drawn at 393 pt device width). This maps the flow's Calendar branch onto concrete screens.

Grounded in: Views/Library/CalendarLibraryView.swift, Components/CalendarHeaderView.swift, CalendarDayCell.swift, DayCard.swift, FoldedDayCardHeader.swift, TimelineRow.swift, TimelineBead.swift, MedicationBarView.swift. Spacing tokens: xs 4 · s 8 · m 12 · l 16 · xl 20 · xxl 24 · section 32 · hero 40. Radius card 16. Metrics: dayHeaderGlyph 40 · timeBead 36 · rowMoodText 17 · rowTime 13 · rowSignal 12 · minTapTarget 44.

How to read these

Static element Tappable region Glyph / icon placeholder Mood tint (a colour in-app; grey here) Selected day Day marker: dark = mood check-in · light = entry only · none = no entry Timeline connector (same day) 1 See notes under the frame

Colour is intentionally removed: mood tint, the medication purple, and the sleep blue are all rendered as neutral greys and called out in the notes. Everything else is greyscale by nature. Card outlines here are a wireframe boundary; in-app the DayCard is a cream surface with no border (DayCard.swift:39).

User stories on this view

C1Browse by week

Scan days in a compact week strip; each day shows a mood-marker dot.

C2Expand to month

Tap the month label / chevron to open the full month; swipe left/right pages months.

C3Glance a day

Each day is a folded DayCard: mood glyph + “Mood · Weekday” + a one-line energy/focus/med summary.

C4Open a day

Tap a card header to expand into the day's individual check-ins as a timeline.

C5Open a check-in

Tap a timeline row (or its chevron) to push the Recording Detail.

C6Jump to a day

Tap a day cell: it fills, the list scrolls to it, others collapse, the selected day auto-expands.

C7No data yet

Empty state below the header: “No entries yet · Record a voice note to see it here.”

C8Active medication

A persistent medication bar pins the active dose + its remaining-time track at the bottom.

Navigation out of this view

The Calendar Library's only push. Tapping a timeline row appends the recording's UUID to the tab's NavigationPath; the .navigationDestination(for: UUID.self) resolves it to Recording Detail. Leaving the Calendar tab clears this path; re-entering jumps to today.

Behavioural variants — same layout, different rule (no separate frame needed):
  1. alwaysExpandCards (Settings): every DayCard renders expanded regardless of per-card state. CalendarLibraryView.swift:14,88
  2. autoExpandOnSelection = false (Settings): tapping a day collapses all cards but does NOT open the selected one. CalendarLibraryView.swift:13,126
  3. Multiple cards open at once: expansion is per-day (ExpandedDayCards.toggling), so more than one card can be expanded simultaneously. ExpandedDayCards.swift:22
  4. Accessibility text size (≥ AX1): the month grid force-collapses to one week and the month label becomes plain text (expand disabled). CalendarHeaderView.swift:20,49
  5. Out-of-month tap: tapping a greyed adjacent-month day pages to that month first, then selects it. CalendarLibraryView.swift:111
  6. Bounded paging: back stops at the earliest month with data; forward stops at the current month. CalendarLibraryView.swift:138
  7. Tab housekeeping: leaving Calendar clears its NavigationPath; entering jumps to today and collapses the calendar. CalendarLibraryView.swift:47-52
  8. Deleted underneath: if the open recording is deleted, the detail push auto-pops back to the list. CalendarLibraryView.swift:41-43
  9. Edge fade: the timeline scroll fades at its bottom edge (edgeFadeMask). CalendarLibraryView.swift:104
