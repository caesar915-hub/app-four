Insights — additional view concepts

Fifteen further candidates, beyond the four already spec'd in 036-insights-evolution-proposal.html. A–I adapt the Daylio / Bearable inventory; J–O come from a wider scan of famous app visualizations (Oura, Whoop, Apple Health, Garmin Body Battery, AutoSleep, Gentler Streak, Strava, How We Feel, Screen Time, WaterMinder). Full sources: docs/competitor-research-insights.md. For visualization only — no commitment. Each concept states what it adapts, the deterministic calculation, and its evidence gate. Numbers come from the same 18-check-in August dataset unless tagged illustrative.
