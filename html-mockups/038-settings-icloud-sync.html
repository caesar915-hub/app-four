Sync with iCloud?

Your journal syncs across your Apple devices, automatically in the background.

  • ✓Your check-ins, transcripts, moods and medication log sync. Audio stays on its device.
  • ✓It goes only to your own iCloud account. No account, no Squirl server.
  • ✓Squirl can’t read it. The developer has no access to your data.
End-to-end encryption is on when you’ve enabled Advanced Data Protection in your Apple Account. Otherwise your data is private to your iCloud account and still unreadable by Squirl.
