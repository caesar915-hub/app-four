Mood-word contrast — what's wrong & how the fix reads

Your folded day-cards, rebuilt in the browser from the actual hexes in MoodLevel+Palette.swift. The contrast numbers are computed live with the real WCAG formula (word color over the color @ 0.24 block tint, composited over the card surface). This is the design-review finding ① made visible — and the proposed column is editable, so the shades you land on are the exact hexes I'll put in the fix.

The rule (Apple HIG + WCAG): normal text needs ≥ 4.5:1 contrast. Right now wordColor = color — the mood word is painted in the same saturated hue as the tint behind it, so it washes out (worst case 1.54:1 in light). The fix is the “deeper, legible shade” your own docstring already promises but never implemented. drag the swatches / type hexes to tune
