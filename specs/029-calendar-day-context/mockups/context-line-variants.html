Same folded day card (a "Great" Monday, two chips + a med), same day on the calendar: Team standup 9:00 · Design review 11:00 · 1:1 Sarah 14:00 · Dentist 16:30 · Mum's birthday (all-day). Only the context line at the bottom of the card differs.

Variant A · titles + overflow
First titles in day order, then “+N”. No classification: what you wrote in your calendar is what you see. Captured fields stay title · times · all-day.

Alike events are folded into a class word (“3 meetings”), distinctive ones keep their name. Needs one extra captured fact per event (has-attendees / busy) to know what a “meeting” is.
